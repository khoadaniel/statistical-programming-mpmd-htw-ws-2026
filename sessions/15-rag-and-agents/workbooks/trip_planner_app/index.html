Trip planner: a team of agents in LangGraph

Workbook 05, Step 8, live: the orchestrator agent reads your request, the city agents work in parallel, the reducer joins their reports, and the recommender agent recommends one city.

What this planner does, and what it needs from you

The outcome

One recommended city for a short, cheap trip from Berlin this coming weekend, with the travel, the bed, the money left per person and the weather for each city you compare.

What it needs from you

  • people: how many travel
  • budget: in euros, for the whole group
  • cities: one to four cities to compare
  • nights: optional, 1 if you do not say

If something is missing

  • The graph pauses and asks you (the Ask user node). It never guesses people, budget or cities.
  • No city named? It asks, and names the cities of our travel guide: Prague, Vienna, Dresden.
  • More than four cities? It asks you to choose four.

One city or several

  • One city agent per city, all at the same time; the reducer joins their reports.
  • One city: the recommender agent says whether it fits the budget. Several: it recommends one.
  • A city outside the guide gets the weather, but its prices are unknown, so it cannot be checked against the budget.
